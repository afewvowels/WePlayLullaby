Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-11-16
  Changes:
    - Spitters in the lure layer (20 tiles) are now entranced too: frozen, with a swirl, and drawn in by the song until they're in the sleep layer. They can't fire while entranced.
    - Spitters inch closer like biters, all the way to point blank (2 tiles, 3 in a vehicle) before they wake.
    - All movement while entranced is scripted, so scuttles stop where they should. Fixes biters closing the gap in a second or two.
    - Entranced enemies won't glide through walls, buildings, trees, rocks or water.
    - Slower, bigger lurches: a scuttle is 2 tiles. A biter that falls asleep 9 tiles out takes about 12-22 s to reach you (behemoths about 9-14 s).
    - Rays now draw above ore, rocks and trees, with additive blending so they look like projected light.
    - Rays are warped as if projected over bumpy ground, with an uneven outer edge. Rotation is baked into the animation so the bumps stay put while the rays sweep across them.

Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-11-16
  Features:
    - First pass: Lullaby personal equipment (2x2, one per grid).
    - Sleep layer: biters and spitters within 9 tiles are held in place and twitch. Each pulse they may scuttle closer; the chance builds every pulse they stay put.
    - Siren song: spitters within 20 tiles walk in without attacking until they fall asleep.
    - Worm hush: worms within 50 tiles stop firing while the song lasts.
    - Full wakes (reached you, left behind, or the song stopped) grant immunity. A missed pulse wakes everyone.
    - Gold twisted sunburst on the ground with a travelling jagged wave, soft gold glow, light blue swirls over entranced enemies.
    - Sounds built from a brass pad: swell-in, crossfading pulses with a sub-bass thrum, turntable run-down and a record scratch.
    - Map settings: immunity after waking (0-60 s, default 30), worms can be re-entranced (default off).
